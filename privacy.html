Privacy Policy

Powder Rush: Snowboard · Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: Powder Rush has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your device. If you buy something, Apple or Google handles the payment, and our purchase service (RevenueCat) keeps an anonymous record so your purchases can be unlocked and restored.

This policy explains what information the Powder Rush: Snowboard app (“the game”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Jacob Grigg.

1. Game progress stays on your device

The game saves your coins, best distances, upgrades, the skins and mountains you've unlocked with coins, daily challenge results and settings (sound, vibration, steering) in your device's storage. This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. In-app purchases

Some skins and mountains, and the Season Pass, can be bought with real money. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To unlock and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, Inc. When the game starts or you buy or restore something, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID created by the game (not linked to your name or Apple/Google account details);
  • your purchase history for this game: which items, when, the price and currency, and the store's transaction IDs;
  • basic technical details: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and country settings, and your IP address (used to work out your country and prices).

This information is used only to verify purchases, unlock what you've bought, restore purchases on a new or reinstalled device, and keep the records stores require. RevenueCat handles it under its own privacy policy.

3. Sharing your score

The Share button opens your device's own share menu with a message about your run. You choose whether and where to send it. We don't receive it.

4. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites, and no use of your device's advertising identifier.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No accounts or logins.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

5. Children

Powder Rush is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only information that leaves the device is the anonymous purchase information described above. Purchases require the permission of the store account holder, and parents can turn off in-app purchases in their device's parental controls (Screen Time on iPhone, Family Link on Android).

6. Keeping and deleting information

Information on your device is kept until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as they're needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal and tax requirements. You can ask us to delete the purchase record RevenueCat holds for you by contacting us (see below). Your purchases stay restorable through the App Store or Google Play.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Because we don't know who you are, please include your purchase receipt or transaction ID from Apple or Google so we can find the right record.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/JacobGrigg14/powder-rush-site/issues. Please don't post receipts or other personal details there; we'll reply with a private way to send them.
